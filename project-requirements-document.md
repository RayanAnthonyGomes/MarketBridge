### 🚀 **Product Vision: What to Build**

- MarketBridge is a web-based, AI-powered digital marketplace designed to connect sellers directly with consumers while eliminating intermediary middlemen.

- The platform aims to provide a centralized digital space to manage product listings, buyer-seller interactions, and transactions.

- It leverages three core Artificial Intelligence modules to create a smarter, fairer experience: a personalized recommendation engine, a dynamic pricing model powered by reinforcement learning, and an anomaly-detection fraud prevention module.

---

### 👥 **Target Audience: Targeted Users**

- **Buyers:** The primary consumers who use the platform to search for products, receive personalized recommendations, manage shopping carts and wishlists, track deliveries, and submit verified reviews.

- **Sellers:** Business users who utilize the platform to create and manage product listings, monitor their inventory, receive AI-driven pricing suggestions, and track their sales performance.

- **Administrators:** The system managers who govern the platform by managing user access, moderating product listings, reviewing fraud alerts, and generating analytical reports.

---

### ⭐ **Key Features (By Core Module)**

**1. User & Product Management**

- Secure account registration, authentication, and role-based access control for Buyers, Sellers, and Admins.

- Tools for sellers to create product listings, upload multiple images, and manage categorizations and inventory.

- Administrative capabilities to approve, reject, or suspend product listings.

**2. Marketplace Operations**

- Robust product search with keyword functionality, filtering, and sorting options.

- Comprehensive cart and order management, including order placement, confirmations, real-time tracking, and processing of returns or refunds.

- A verified product review system where buyers can submit ratings.

**3. AI Recommendation & Dynamic Pricing**

- An AI recommendation engine that analyzes user browsing and purchasing behavior to display personalized and trending products.

- An AI pricing tool that evaluates market demand and competitor pricing to offer dynamic pricing suggestions to sellers.

- Controls allowing sellers to set minimum and maximum price boundaries for these AI suggestions.

**4. Fraud Detection & Security**

- Continuous automated monitoring of transactions to detect suspicious user behavior and identify fake reviews.

- Automatic generation of fraud alerts and risk notifications for platform administrators.

- A dedicated case management interface for administrators to review and resolve flagged fraud cases.

**5. Dashboards, Analytics & Notifications**

- Personalized, role-specific dashboards customized for the unique needs of Buyers, Sellers, and Admins.

- Generation of analytical and operational reports for platform governance.

- A system for sending real-time notifications and emails regarding order updates, stock changes, pricing alerts, and system announcements.
